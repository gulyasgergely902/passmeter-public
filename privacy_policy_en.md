# Privacy Policy for PassMeter

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026

PassMeter ("the App") is designed with a privacy-first approach. We believe that your personal data belongs to you, and we build our software accordingly.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
PassMeter does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics.

* **Local Storage:** All pass details (pass name, start and expiry date, total and available entry count, expiry reminder, icon name and color), and preferences created within the App are stored exclusively on your device using local iOS storage mechanisms (SwiftData).
* **No External Servers:** We do not operate external servers, databases, or user accounts. Your data never leaves your device.

## 2. Notifications
If you grant permission for notifications, PassMeter uses iOS Local Notifications to alert you on your device before a pass expires. These notifications are generated entirely locally by iOS on your device. No remote servers or push notification services (APNs) are used to track or send notifications.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics
PassMeter does **not** integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks.

## 4. App Store Data
When downloading or purchasing the App, Apple may collect data according to their own Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your payment information or personal Apple ID details.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
